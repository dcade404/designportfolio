Designing a Better Booking Experience

I redesigned how add-on services are managed and recommended in the booking flow—making it easier for businesses to control offerings across locations and for customers to discover custom recommendations, right when and where it matters.

Project Overview

Contribution

Principle UX Designer, Interaction Design

Target Users

Analysts, Service Providers, Clients

Products

Service Applications, Consumer-facing Apps (B2B and B2C)

Outcome

Add-on adoption achieved a rate of 40%, reflecting the success of integrating personalized booking options. Operational efficiency improved with a 20% reduction in manual effort for front desk staff.

Impact

The platform significantly improved service adoption and operational efficiency.

Solution

Boulevard Labs' Booking Agent is a sophisticated scheduling and booking application designed for the personal care industry, enabling businesses to efficiently manage and integrate add-on services.

Service Management

Centralized tools for managing and configuring add-on services across multiple locations.

Consumer Booking Experience

Streamlined booking process with personalized service recommendations.

Front Desk Tools

Help front desk staff identify cross-sell and upsell opportunities

Admin Console

Powerful tools for managing locations, services, and staff.

Consumer-Level Online Booking Enhancements:

During the online booking process, customers are presented with relevant add-on services that complement their primary service selection, such as suggesting a blowout when booking a haircut. An alias set at the business level is presented unless the location has chosen to be customized further.

Business Value

Revenue Growth

Increased service adoption and upsell opportunities through personalized recommendations.

Operational Efficiency

Streamlined processes and reduced manual effort through automated recommendations.
