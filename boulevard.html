Boulevard: Cross-platform Recommendations and Administration

In the personal care industry, businesses face challenges in effectively integrating and promoting add-on services within their service offerings. Currently, there is a lack of a streamlined system that allows for both centralized management of these services across multiple locations and personalized customer experiences during the booking process. This gap results in missed opportunities for upselling and cross-selling, ultimately impacting the overall revenue and customer satisfaction.

Contribution: Principle UX Designer, Interaction Design

Target Users: Analysts, Service Providers, Clients

Products: Service Applications, Consumer-facing Apps (B2B and B2C)

Solution Summary

Boulevard Labs’ Booking Agent is a sophisticated scheduling and booking application designed for the personal care industry. The application enables businesses to efficiently manage and integrate add-on services to base services, enhancing the customer booking experience and increasing business revenue through upselling and cross-selling opportunities.

Target Users

Owners, managers, and operators of personal care businesses, such as salons, spas, and wellness centers, who need centralized tools to manage and configure add-on services across multiple locations. These users prioritize operational efficiency, revenue growth through upselling, and consistency in customer offerings.

Individual customers booking personal care services who value personalized and seamless booking experiences. These users benefit from transparent and intuitive options to customize their services with add-ons that meet their specific needs, enhancing satisfaction and loyalty.

Key Features

Business-Level Configuration and Customization: Businesses can configure and define add-on services via Centralized Add-On Management UI, ensuring consistency across all locations.

Cascading overrides empower locations with flexibility and control by enabling them to disable business-configured add-on services, customize key attributes such as alias name and description, and introduce unique, location-specific add-on services. This ensures a tailored and consistent offering while meeting the specific needs of individual locations.

Consumer-Level Online Booking Enhancements:

During the online booking process, customers are presented with relevant add-on services that complement their primary service selection, such as suggesting a blowout when booking a haircut.

Business-Level Booking Flow Improvements:

The application supports front desk staff by suggesting potential cross-sell and upsell opportunities during the booking and check-in processes, ensuring that every customer interaction is maximized for revenue potential.

Measurable Outcomes

Boulevard Online Booking utilized a heuristic approach to recommend add-on services based on popular combinations and staff input. As the application gathered more data on customer preferences and behaviors, machine learning models were deployed to enable smarter, personalized recommendations, enhancing the upselling process and further tailoring the customer experience.

The project delivered measurable improvements across several KPIs — demonstrating clear business and operational impact. The upsell conversion rate increased by 25% over three months, while average revenue per booking rose from $50 to $65, driven by the implementation of targeted cross-sell and upsell strategies.

Add-on adoption achieved a rate of 40%, reflecting the success of integrating personalized booking options. Operational efficiency improved with a 20% reduction in manual effort for front desk staff, facilitated by automated suggestions for upsells and cross-sells. Additionally, customer retention increased by 15% within six months, directly attributed to the introduction of personalized booking experiences.
